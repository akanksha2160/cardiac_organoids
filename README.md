
# Cardiac Organoids Early Prediction Project

## Overview

In this project, my team and I at National Cheng Kung University are developing a model to predict the early beating behavior of cardiac organoids. We aim to leverage advanced deep learning techniques to create an accurate and robust predictive model. Our goal is to contribute to the understanding and early detection of functional properties in cardiac organoids, which could have significant implications for research and therapeutic applications.

## Objectives

- Develop a predictive model for early detection of beating behavior in cardiac organoids.
- Explore various deep learning methods and architectures to optimize prediction accuracy.
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary experts to refine and validate the model.

## Approach

Our approach involves:

1. **Data Collection:** Gathering data from cardiac organoids to identify key features related to their beating behavior.
2. **Model Development:** Experimenting with different deep learning models to identify the most effective architecture for prediction.
3. **Validation:** Testing and validating the model against new datasets to ensure its reliability and accuracy.
4. **Collaboration:** Working closely with experts in the field to ensure that our model aligns with the latest research and clinical needs.

## Team

This project is a collaborative effort between researchers at National Cheng Kung University, bringing together expertise in bioengineering, data science, and deep learning.
